I am reproducing the findings of Bartel et al. (npj Computational Materials, 2020), which critically examines whether ML models that accurately predict formation energy (ΔHf) can also reliably predict thermodynamic stability (ΔHd). This work involves implementing all six compositional models (Roost, ElemNet, Meredig, Magpie, AutoMat, ElFrac) on roughly 154,000 Materials Project compounds, reconstructing the thermodynamic stability evaluation pipeline using convex hull analysis, and examining the relationship between formation energy prediction accuracy and stability classification performance.
